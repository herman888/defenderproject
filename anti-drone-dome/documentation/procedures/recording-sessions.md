# Development recording procedure

All clips are **DEVELOPMENT CLIPS, NOT HELD-OUT EVALUATION DATA**. Media remains
outside git; each clip has a tracked JSON sidecar with mode, measured FOV,
focus, lighting, UTC timestamp, commit, operator notes, and known distances.

Capture: a 60-second static indoor scene; several minutes of a hand-moved small
object at varied speed/distance; and at least five minutes of daylight sky
footage, including near-but-never-at-sun framing and a horizon transition. If a
driver-exposed IR control is later verified, repeat the indoor scene both ways.
